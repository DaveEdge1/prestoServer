Step 1 — Pick primary proxies per dataset

Each LiPD dataset usually contributes a canonical proxy record plus several auxiliary series (uncertainties, metadata columns, secondary measurements). Presto keeps the records that pass all three of the filters below (interpretation variables, valid proxy variables by archive, and optionally compilation membership) — records that fail any filter are excluded. Tweak the filters to broaden or narrow selection; any dataset that falls out entirely is listed so you can spot accidental over-filtering. Click Next → location review when you're done.

Step 2 — Review duplicates by location

Proxy records that survived step 1 are now compared across datasets using:
  • Spatial proximity — records within 10 km of each other are candidates.
  • Variable match — only records sharing the same proxy variable (e.g. d18O) are grouped.
  • Compilation exclusion — records from the same named compilation are assumed to be intentionally distinct.
  • Temporal overlap — records whose age ranges do not overlap are never grouped.
For each flagged group, expand it to load time series plots and similarity metrics (Pearson r, DTW distance) and pick which record(s) to carry forward.

No duplicate candidates to review

Presto did not find any pairs of records that meet the duplicate-candidate criteria. This commonly happens when:

  • your selection draws from a single compilation (records inside one compilation are assumed to be intentionally distinct);
  • your records are spatially well-separated (no two within 10 km share the same variable);
  • your filters narrowed the selection to a small set with no overlapping age ranges.

There is nothing to hand-review on this step — the temporal coverage and PCA panels below remain useful for sanity-checking the selection. You can Continue directly to the editor.

Auto-selection filters

▼

Per-dataset primary proxies are auto-selected by combining three filters with AND logic. Records that pass all enabled filters are kept; the rest are excluded. Tweak any filter to re-run selection live.

Interpretation variables

A record passes if any of its interp_Vars values is checked. Unannotated records are grouped into (no interpretation).

Valid proxy variables (per archive)

Variables from the default priority list are pre-checked. Expand any archive to broaden or narrow.

Compilation membership

Checked against paleoData_mostRecentCompilations. Off by default; enabling it restricts selection to records that belong to at least one compilation, useful for reconstructions that want to lean on curator-vetted data only.

Records from higher-ranked compilations will be kept. Records from lower-ranked (or unlisted) compilations will be removed when they are exact duplicates (Pearson r = 1) of a higher-ranked record. Drag to reorder, or use the ▲ ▼ buttons.
