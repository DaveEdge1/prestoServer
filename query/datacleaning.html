How potential duplicates are identified

Proxy records in your selection are compared using the following criteria to flag groups that may represent the same underlying measurement:
  • Spatial proximity — records within 10 km of each other are considered candidates.
  • Variable match — only records sharing the same proxy variable (e.g. d18O) are grouped.
  • Compilation exclusion — records from the same named compilation are assumed to be intentionally distinct and are never grouped.
  • Temporal overlap — records whose age ranges do not overlap cannot represent the same measurement and are never grouped.
For each flagged group, expand it to load time series plots and similarity metrics (Pearson r, DTW distance). Use the Keep / Remove controls to choose which records to carry forward, then click Save to collapse the group. When you are finished, click Continue at the bottom of the page.

Rank your preferred compilations, then let Presto automatically remove records that are exact (or near) duplicates of a higher-ranked record — so you only have to hand-review the groups that actually need your judgement.
