To create a Bayesian global mean surface temperature (BayGMST) reconstruction, adjust the options below or keep the published defaults. BayGMST fits a Bayesian hierarchical model (STAN) to reduced proxy predictors derived from your selected PAGES 2k records, calibrated against the instrumental record. Choose the proxy type and reduced-proxy method, the reconstruction window, the Stan sampler controls, and the CO2/volcanic forcing transforms.
