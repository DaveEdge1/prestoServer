<p style="font-size:15px">To create a Temperature 12k Composites reconstruction, adjust the options below and click "submit." You will receive an email when your reconstruction is done.
<br>
<br>
This recreates the multi-method Holocene global mean surface temperature (GMST) of <a href="https://doi.org/10.1038/s41597-020-0530-7" target="_blank">Kaufman et al., 2020</a>. Up to five composite methods are applied to the Temperature 12k proxy database &mdash; Standard Calibrated Composite (SCC), Dynamic Calibrated Composite (DCC), Generalized Additive Model (GAM), Composite Plus Scale (CPS) and Pairwise Comparison (PaiCo) &mdash; each compositing records within six 30&deg; latitude bands and area-weighting them into a global mean. Age uncertainty is propagated with a Banded Age Model (5%) and proxy-temperature uncertainty from the per-proxy-type values in the paper. By default all five methods run and their ensembles are pooled into the multi-method consensus GMST (the paper's headline result); toggle methods off to reconstruct with a subset.
<br>
<br>
For more information about each option, mouse over the question-mark icons. Leaving an option at its default is a good choice if you are unsure.<p>
