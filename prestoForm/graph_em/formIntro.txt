<p style="font-size:15px">To create a Graph-EM reconstruction, adjust the options below and click "submit." You will receive an email when your reconstruction is done.
<br>
<br>
For more information about each of the options, mouse over the question mark icons or see <a href="https://projecteuclid.org/journals/annals-of-applied-statistics/volume-9/issue-1/Statistical-paleoclimate-reconstructions-via-Markov-random-fields/10.1214/14-AOAS794.full" target="_blank">Guillot et al. 2015</a> for more details. Default values will produce a reconstruction similar to the one in <a href="https://fzhu2e.github.io/cfr/notebooks/graphem-cli.html" target="_blank">this tutorial</a>. If you are unsure what an option does, leaving it at its default value is a good choice.<p>
