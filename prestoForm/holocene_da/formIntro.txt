<p style="font-size:15px">To create a Holocene reconstruction, adjust the options below and click "submit." You will receive an email when your reconstruction is done.
<br>
<br>
For more information about each of the options, mouse over the question mark icons or see <a href="https://cp.copernicus.org/articles/18/2599/2022/" target="_blank">Erb et al. 2022</a> for more details. Default values will produce a reconstruction similar to the one in Erb et al., 2022. If you are unsure what an option does, leaving it at its default value is a good choice.<p>
